# Excel Business Services

Practical spreadsheet support for small businesses.

I help organise business data and make everyday record-keeping easier with Microsoft Excel.

## Services

- **Spreadsheet cleaning:** organise inconsistent records, standardise formats, identify duplicates and flag missing information.
- **Sales reports and dashboards:** summarise revenue, orders, average order value and product performance.
- **Business trackers:** create simple inventory, order and expense trackers with formulas and data validation.
- **PDF/CSV conversion:** turn suitable text-based records into structured spreadsheets.

## Working Process

1. Share the task and a sample or anonymised copy of the data.
2. We agree on scope, deliverables, price and deadline before work begins.
3. I complete the spreadsheet and check formulas and outputs.
4. You review the agreed deliverables; one in-scope revision is included.

## Scope and Data Care

- I confirm the row, worksheet and complexity limits before quoting.
- I do not silently discard questionable records; I flag them for clarification.
- Please do not send sensitive customer or payment information unless it is necessary and you have permission to share it.
- I do not claim that spreadsheet work is an audit or certified financial review.
